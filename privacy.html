Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

This starter version of CreatorLaunch provides browser-based text utilities. Text entered into the title checker, description counter, hashtag generator, and tag starter is processed locally in your browser by the page scripts. This version does not transmit that text to a CreatorLaunch server.

The hosting provider may process basic technical information such as IP address and request logs to deliver the website. If analytics, advertising, embedded services, cookies, or a contact form are added, this policy must be updated before those features go live to explain the relevant data practices and choices.

Do not enter passwords, financial details, or other sensitive personal information into these tools. For privacy questions, use the support contact published on the live website once configured.
